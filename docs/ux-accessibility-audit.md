# Trainee User Guide

## 1. Getting Started in 3 Clicks
1. **Login**: Navigate to the application URL. Enter your assigned Passcode (e.g. `K7QM-4PXD`) and your Display Name. Click **Start Training**.
2. **Select Track**: Choose either **Sales Mastery** or **Leadership & Difficult Conversations**.
3. **Choose Scenario**: Browse scenario cards filtered by difficulty (1 to 5) or topic. Click on any scenario to review the situation brief, counterpart role, and skills evaluated. Click **Begin Scenario**.

## 2. Navigating the Conversation
- **Text Chat**:
  - Type in the input area and press `Enter` to send.
  - The AI counterpart responds with dynamic pacing, realistic emotion, and conversational pushback.
  - Watch the timer and turn limit at the top of the interface.
- **Voice Mode**:
  - Allow microphone permissions.
  - Speak naturally. Use push-to-talk or hands-free voice activity detection.
  - You can interrupt (barge-in) when the counterpart is speaking if you need to redirect the conversation.
- **Ending the Conversation**:
  - The scenario ends naturally when an agreement, refusal, or clear next step is achieved.
  - You may also click **End Conversation** at any time.

## 3. Reading Your Feedback Report
Upon completing the scenario:
- **Overall Score**: Weighted rating (0-100) combining all evaluated dimensions.
- **Skill Breakdown**: Score (1 to 5) per skill with specific rubric justifications.
- **Key Moments**: 3+ specific dialogue turns with exact quotes, better alternative phrasing, and psychological explanations ("why this works better").
- **Counterpart Reveal**: Discloses what the counterpart was internally thinking or hiding.
- **Actionable Drills**: 3 to 4 concrete practice exercises to prepare for real-world interactions.
